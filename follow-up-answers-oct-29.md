# Follow-up answers: Kettle & Stone (updated Oct 29)

This replaces the Oct 28 file. New answers since then are marked (new).

Builders have asked good questions this week. Here are all the answers so far in one place, shared with everyone. Treat them like the rest of your discovery materials: as evidence, not instructions.

Where a person doesn't know something, that is the answer. Don't fill the gap with a guess; note it as an open question and keep your work easy to change.

## The Friday demo (from the program lead)

**What counts as a match?**
A match is any of your names being on Tanya's list for that opening, or showing nobody when her answer is nobody. The order of your names doesn't count. Every name you show must be on her list.

**If more than three people fit, do any three of them count?**
Yes. If the names you show are all on Tanya's list for that opening, it counts as a match. Showing fewer than three is fine too.

**Can Friday's openings include ones where nobody fits?**
Yes. Like the sample, some openings can have nobody as the right answer.

**What files do we get on Friday?** (new)
The same kind of input files as now: a newer booking export, the waitlist as it is that week, and the late openings. Tanya's answers aren't handed out; your output is checked against them on the call.

**Is the 70% target real refills, or how accurate the tool is?** (new)
Both matter, in different ways. On Friday, the done checks are judged on your tool: its names against Tanya's answers on new openings. The 70% is the clinic's real result, which depends on more than the list.

**Will Friday's waitlist keep the same line numbers?**
Tanya: "It's my book, so I add to the end. Same columns, same handwriting."

## Tanya Boudreau, front desk lead

**How is the waitlist kept today?**
"It's a notebook by the phone. Bethany and the therapists write in it too. The file you have is me typing it up on October 21, in the order it's written."

**When do late cancellations come in, and how do you find out?**
"Mostly at night. Online at eleven or two in the morning, or a voicemail at six-thirty. Or an hour before. Hardly ever when I'm sitting there with time. I find out when I look at the schedule or play the voicemails."

**Can anyone act on a cancellation before 10:30?** (new)
"Nobody, honestly. On weekdays it's just me at the desk, and I'm on the calls from eight. Bethany only does Saturdays, and she's not in till 8:30. Marc looks at Kneadly at night, but he's not phoning anyone."

**Who keeps the waitlist up to date?** (new)
"Me, supposedly. But Bethany and the therapists add people too, and nobody takes anyone out." Told that Marc says it's part of the desk job: "Then it has to take less time than the book, not more."

**Would a therapist like Josée accept a note about a possible fit?** (new)
"In theory. I don't have time to run to Josée between clients and ask. Kelsey and Dave will take anyone; Josée wants to see who it is."

**Is there any record of who said yes or no to past offers?** (new)
Not known. Nobody has mentioned keeping one.

**When more than one person fits an opening, who gets the first offer?**
"Whoever asked first, I think. That's what I've always done. But Marc says fill it, so maybe whoever can get here fastest? I'd need to be able to explain it to someone who asks. Can you show me what each way would look like?"

## Marc Cormier, owner (new)

**Who gets the first call?**
"My regulars, sure. And the other therapists would say theirs. Look, fill the table. You and Tanya figure out the order." Nobody has decided this; it's the client's call.

**Can a therapist say no to who fills their slot?**
"Yes. It's their table. Each of them gets a say."

**What would you pay each month, and who looks after it?**
"If it fills two more a week, fifty a month, I wouldn't blink. A hundred and fifty, no. And Julie can't babysit it."

**Could something go out at 7 a.m., before anyone is at the desk?**
"Something going out at seven with nobody looking? Show me what it says and who it goes to. Not a yes, not this week."

**What would you need before client data goes to an outside service?**
"Put it on one page. I'm not signing up for something I don't understand."
